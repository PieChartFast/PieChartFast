Create Your Chart in 3 Simple Steps

Relax and enjoy the best free pie chart maker on the web. With no logins, no watermarks, and no sign-ups required, your chart will automatically update as you work.

1

Enter Your Data & Labels

Type your labels and numbers into the data input rows. Feel free to adjust the data at any time—your chart updates automatically. You can easily add or remove slices using the "+ Add Item" button or the garbage can symbol. Use the Value Unit field to describe your unit type (like Miles, Hours, Days, or USD), or feel free to leave it blank.

2

Customize Your Design & Colors

Give your chart a professional look instantly. Toggle between a traditional pie chart and a modern donut style. Select from curated options like Clean Corporate or Vibrant, or use the monochrome palette for printing on a black and white laser printer. Want complete control? By clicking the color circle next to any slice's row, you can choose a custom color that perfectly matches your branding.

3

Select Your Options & Export

Don't forget the options at the bottom of the control panel! Choose how to display your slice labels: checking Percent % will automatically show the percentage of the total for the pie chart. You can also toggle the legend or choose a Transparent Background—which exports your pie chart with no background, perfect for dropping seamlessly into your projects. When ready, download your high-resolution PNG or JPG instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this pie chart maker free to use?

Yes, PieChartFast is 100% free. You can create, customize, and download unlimited high-resolution charts without any hidden fees, subscriptions, or watermarks.

Can I pick custom colors for individual slices?

Absolutely! While we offer beautifully curated color palettes like Pastel and Vibrant, you can fully customize any individual slice. Simply click the color circle right next to the slice's row in the data panel to open a color picker and select your exact hue.

How do I prepare a chart for printing on a black and white printer?

If you are printing your document or presentation on a standard black and white laser printer, color gradients can sometimes blend together. We recommend selecting our Monochrome palette in the design section. It applies distinct shades of gray, ensuring your chart remains perfectly legible in print.

What does the Transparent Background option do?

When you turn on the Transparent Background switch at the bottom of the panel, the tool removes the solid white background box from your final image. This allows you to export your pie chart with a clear background, making it perfect for layering directly into presentation slides, dark-themed websites, or graphic design projects without any awkward white borders.

How do I add or remove categories from my chart?

Managing your data is incredibly simple. To add a new category, click the "+ Add Item" button at the bottom of your data grid. If you need to remove a category, just click the garbage can symbol next to that specific row, and the chart will instantly recalculate the remaining data.
